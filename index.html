01 — THE STORY

Every beginning
has a first build.

Bob was Capstone Robotics' first robot—and an important part of how the team got started.

His first major appearance was at K-Days in Edmonton, Alberta, where he helped introduce students and the community to robotics, engineering, and STEM education.
